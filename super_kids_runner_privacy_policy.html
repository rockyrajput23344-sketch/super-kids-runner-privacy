Privacy Policy for Super Kids Runner

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Super Kids Runner ("the App") handles information when you use it.

Information handled by the App

The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or payment details to play.

The App may store gameplay settings and progress locally on your device. This information may remain subject to your device's storage settings.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The App integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK to support the display of advertisements. During development, the App may use Google's test ad units. The advertising configuration may change in a future release, and this policy will be updated if necessary.

Depending on the SDK version, configuration, and how advertising services are used, Google Mobile Ads may collect and share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • Advertising and other device or account identifiers;
  • Ad and app interaction information, such as app launches, taps, and video views;
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be processed for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related service purposes. Data handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit according to Google's published disclosures.

Google's data practices are described in its Privacy Policy and its Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Children's privacy

Super Kids Runner is a family-friendly game that may be offered to children and adults. The App does not ask players to submit personal information directly to the developer to play.

Third-party SDKs may process device or usage information as described in this policy. The developer must configure advertising and other SDKs appropriately for the App's declared audience and comply with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements. This policy does not itself guarantee compliance with those requirements.

Third-party services

The App is distributed through Google Play and integrates Google Mobile Ads. Google and other service providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies to provide their services.

Data security and retention

Gameplay information stored locally is subject to your device's storage management. Information processed by third-party services is subject to those providers' retention and security practices. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features, SDKs, advertising configuration, or data practices change. Updates will be published at this same URL with a revised date.
